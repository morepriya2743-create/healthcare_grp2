Laboratory Test Details

Test Name

HbA1c (Hemoglobin A1c)

Purpose

The HbA1c test measures the average blood sugar level over the past 2 to 3 months. It is used to diagnose and monitor Diabetes Mellitus.

Sample Required

Blood sample collected from a vein.

Preparation Instructions

Important Instructions:
• Inform your doctor about any medications you are taking.
• Stay hydrated before the test.
• Wear comfortable clothing for easy blood collection.

Fasting Requirement

Fasting is NOT required for the HbA1c test.

Normal Reference Range

Category Reference Range
Normal Below 5.7%
Prediabetes 5.7% – 6.4%
Diabetes 6.5% or higher

Report Delivery Time

Reports are generally available within 24 hours.
*Delivery time may vary depending on laboratory workload.
