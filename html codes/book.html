🔬 Diagnostic Test Details

Everything you need to know before your laboratory test.

🩸 Complete Blood Count (CBC)

A commonly performed blood test used to evaluate overall blood health.

🧪 Sample

Venous Blood

⏱ Report Time

24–48 Hours

🍽 Fasting

Generally Not Required

🎯 Purpose

A Complete Blood Count (CBC) evaluates important components of blood and may help identify conditions such as anemia, infections and other blood-related problems.

🧪 Sample Required

A venous blood sample is collected by a trained laboratory professional.

📋 Preparation Instructions

No special preparation is generally required for a CBC.

Patients should follow any specific instructions provided by their doctor or laboratory.

📊 Normal Reference Range

Hemoglobin: 12–16 g/dL
White Blood Cells: 4,000–11,000 cells/μL
Platelets: 150,000–450,000 cells/μL

Reference ranges may vary depending on laboratory methods, age and other factors.

📄 Report Delivery Time

The CBC report is generally available within 24–48 hours.

Reports may be collected from the laboratory or accessed through the available online reporting service.

⚠️ Important Instructions

Please inform the laboratory staff about any medications or special instructions provided by your doctor.

Follow all instructions provided by the laboratory before sample collection.

️ Fasting Requirements

Fasting is generally not required for a CBC test.

However, if the CBC is being performed along with tests such as a lipid profile or blood glucose test, additional fasting requirements may apply.

If you are unsure about preparation requirements, please confirm them with your healthcare professional.
