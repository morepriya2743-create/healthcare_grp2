📋 Common Laboratory Tests

🩸 CBC
A CBC is a blood test that measures red blood cells, white blood cells, hemoglobin, and platelets. It helps identify infections, anemia, and many other health conditions.

🍬 HbA1c
HbA1c is a blood test that shows the average blood sugar level over the past 2–3 months. It is commonly used to diagnose and monitor diabetes.

🫀 LFT
LFT is a group of blood tests that checks how well the liver is functioning. It helps detect liver diseases, infections, and possible liver damage.

🫘 KFT
KFT is a group of blood tests that evaluates kidney function. It measures substances such as creatinine and urea to help detect kidney-related disorders.

❤️ ECG
An ECG records the electrical activity of the heart. It helps doctors identify irregular heartbeats, heart attacks, and other heart-related conditions.
